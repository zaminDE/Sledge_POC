    How the numbers are defined

    Active company: one or more actions by the company's own users in a Monday to Sunday week (UTC). Work done by the Sledge agent or by an outside party, such as a vendor signing a waiver, does not count.

    Document processed: a distinct document, counted once in the week it first appears.

    Received to paid: for each paid bill, the time between its first "received" event and its first "paid" event. Unpaid bills are not in the average; they show as awaiting payment.
